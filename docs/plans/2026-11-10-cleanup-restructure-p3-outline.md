## 3-3 in one paragraph

Sixteen tasks. The WAILA panel asks a block through `waila.Provider` (and `waila.Adapter` for vanilla blocks), so `look` imports `core` and `naming` only and sits in the core layer; `expand` hides the panel through `look.Hidden`, `tinker` and `wand` follow it through `look.Follow`. An item says its slot decoration through `slotdeco.Decorated`. The creative list is put together from `core.Feature.Creative` with explicit slots, in today's order. A feature registers its commands through `core.Feature.Commands`. The root's `names.go` goes to `naming`, autofill becomes its own package. Two new goldens (`look.golden`, 143 panels; `creative.golden`, 243 items) pin the output first. Left as they are, with reasons in the reference: the operator tools and `giveItems`, `itemsFeature`, the root's `hopper.go`, the copier's and the codex's knowledge of other features, the arena and screen helpers of `core`.

- The root has 92 test files (88 at HEAD plus `pin_look_test.go`, `pin_creative_test.go`, `commands_test.go`, `decor_test.go`).

## 3-4 in one paragraph

Thirteen tasks. Kind identity stays in `machine` by the owner's decision (names, ids, `Order`, block and item registration, tier and shape tables): registration order is in the wire goldens. A kind of another feature names its feature in `Def.Owner`, and the feature hands the kind's `machine.Behaviour` (click, run, item I/O, slot rule, panel lines, info line) to `machine.Register` from its `Setup`; `core.Feature` gets `Check`, and `machine`'s check stops `Install` when an owner did not register. `storage` (3 kinds), `farm` (1), `dimension` (2) and `netstore` (9) register theirs; the frame's kind checks for them become hooks or three flags of the definition (`NoDisplay`, `NoWindow`, `Cycle`); the four methods of the client definition follow one `shapeOf`; the hooks end up private behind `Register`. A rarity's colour moves to `common/slotdeco` and `decor` to the core layer. One kind cannot go home: with `tinker` importing `machine` the machines initialise before the tinker's tools and 512 entries of `items.golden` change, so `machine/tinkertable.go` sets the tinker table's behaviour and stays the one feature import of `machine`, guarded by `TestMachineImportsNoFeature`. A new golden (`kinds.golden`, 145 lines) pins every kind's click, info icon and slots first. Left as they are, with reasons in the reference: `machine/netshape.go`, the owners' own switches over their kinds, the break and place subscribers, `runMachine` and the other switches over `machine`'s own twenty kinds.

- The root has 95 test files (93 at a31f0d6 plus `pin_kinds_test.go` and `kinds_test.go`), about 18,700 lines.

## 3-5 in one paragraph

Twenty tasks. First two support packages, `core/coretest` (a session on a synchronous world as `core.Lifecycle`, a player, a transaction, ticks, and `Setup`, which sets a feature up once per test binary) and `machine/machinetest` (placing a machine), with the root's helpers as wrappers over them and no test moved. Then one package per task, from the leaves up, `machine` last: `elevator`, `handwork`, `copier`, `tinker`, `island`, `achievement` with `decor`, `fairy`, `netstore`, `storage`, `farm`, `conduit`, `dimension`, `climate`, `wand`, `expand`, `screens`, `machine` (two tasks). A moved test is in the external package `<pkg>_test`, changes only its package clause and the names of the helpers it calls, and its package sets its own feature up in `setup_test.go` (`conduit` also `network`). A test that needs the recipes or the research of `catalog`, the hopper the root registers, a second feature, an item another package registers, or an unexported root identifier stays in the root; when that is one to three tests of a file whose rest moves, the file is split by top-level declaration (14 files). The last task writes the documents and two guards: the support packages are imported by test files only, and a package's tests import neither the root nor, below the content lists, `catalog`, `codex` or `screens`, and set features up only through `coretest.Setup`. No production file changes and no golden is touched.
