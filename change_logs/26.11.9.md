# 26.11.9 (2026-11-12)

## Second dimension
- A hunter that takes a killing hit in the second dimension now dies and respawns at once on the island, instead of being pulled out just before the hit lands. The inventory is kept, and the death counts for the death advancement.
- Rewards that do not fit the inventory of a hunter that died lie where the hunter comes back to, not where it died.
- An explosion that kills a hunter no longer throws it.
- A player who was in the second dimension when the server stopped is brought back to the island before the join items are handed out.
- Island expansion mode and the modes of the second dimension no longer nest.
- The two refusal messages and the goal help call the place the second dimension.

## Blocks broken by an explosion
- A machine blown up leaves the machine monitor's list.
- A drawer blown up loses its display and its label, and a drawer set no longer reaches past a trim that was blown up.
- A storage network node blown up drops its filter setting, and the cables it leaves behind become networks of their own.

## Tools and advancements
- The config copier, the construction wands, the hammers and the dimension stone do their work on the air through the item itself.
- A cancelled move and a cancelled hit are not counted for advancements.
- The menu item gets its slot before any other join step hands out items.

## Under the hood
- The content was restructured around one kernel (`content/skygear/core`): features subscribe to events in six tiers, no subscriber stops another, and a cancelled event reaches only the subscribers that ask for it. What a block or an item can do itself moved out of the event subscribers into the block or the item.
- The Dragonfly patch carries less: what could be built outside it was moved out.
