# 26.11.8 (2026-11-10)

## New features
- Added the solar generator and the hydro generator. The solar generator makes up to 8 RE/t from sunlight while only air and glass are above it: full at noon, about a fifth at dawn and dusk, none at night, 40% in rain and 60% in snow. The hydro generator makes up to 10 RE/t from the water flowing on its faces: falling water gives the most and still water gives nothing. Both take upgrade kits and show their rate, and why they stop, in the info panel.
- Added compressed blocks. Nine cobblestone in a 3x3 make compressed cobblestone, and nine of those the next level, up to level 4 (6,561 cobblestone). Gravel, sand and dirt compress up to level 2. One compressed block on its own crafts back into nine. The "Cobblestone Compression" and "Gravel, Sand and Dirt Compression" research unlock them.
- Island chunks can be paid for with compressed cobblestone, which counts as the cobblestone it holds. Loose cobblestone is used first, the change comes back in lower levels and cobblestone, and change that does not fit goes to Lost and Found.
- Added the network conduit. It goes into a conduit bundle next to the energy, item and fluid conduits and joins storage network devices as the network cable does. Six conduit binders and three network cables make eight, unlocked by the storage network research.

## Silkworms
- Infested leaves are now silkworm leaves with a cocoon that grows 10% every 6 seconds, so a cocoon is full in a minute. The info panel shows "Cocoon N%".
- From 20% the worms spread to a leaf next to them, and a full cocoon spreads to every leaf around it.
- Silkworm leaves decay with the tree when no log is left within four blocks, as leaves do. Infested leaves from 26.11.7 turn into a full cocoon.
- Removed the "String" research, which unlocked nothing. The sieve, archery and wool research now follow the wooden crook, and the String advancement follows the Silkworm advancement.

## Construction wand
- Sneaking and right-clicking the air opens a radial menu instead of a settings window. Each sector of the ring is a setting with its current value, a press changes it at once, and one sector undoes the last placement. The pointer, touch, the arrow keys, WASD and the number keys all work.
- The preview shows the blocks themselves under green glass instead of outlines. The crosshair and clicks go through the preview to the block behind it.
- The iron hammer's vein preview shows blue glass on the blocks it would break.

## Machines
- The harvester, item vacuum and tree feller can move the centre of their work area away from the machine, on a new page of the work area screen. The range box follows it.
- The item vacuum takes items lying on the floor of its area.
- The info panel shows a machine's output and a flow hopper's contents as item slots with their counts instead of lines of text.
- Machine items stack whether or not they have been through a save.
- The drawer's menu is a small window that fits its slots.
- Bamboo on a drawer front is drawn flat, as a stick is.
- Opening the controller of a storage network that has a network conduit lists the conduit by its name.

## Removed
- Removed the bridging assist with its setting, help page and crosshair mark. Vanilla places blocks better.

## Operators
- `/sgadmin give` completes every vanilla item and takes a name without its namespace when only one namespace has it. `/sgadmin item` is removed.
