# 26.11.7 (2026-11-10)

## New features
- Added crooks. There are wooden, stone, iron and diamond crooks, each unlocked by research. Breaking leaves with a crook drops saplings more often (20% bare-handed, from 30% with a wooden crook up to 45% with a diamond one) and sometimes drops a silkworm. Each leaf costs 1 durability. The behaviour and textures come from the Ex Nihilo Fabrico mod (MIT).
- Added silkworms and infested leaves. Using a silkworm on leaves turns them into infested leaves over four stages, and the infestation spreads to nearby leaves. Breaking infested leaves drops string, and a crook drops more. Infestation does not spread outside the island's chunks.
- Added cooked silkworms. Cook a silkworm in a furnace, smoker or campfire; eating one restores 5 hunger.

## Handwork
- Four bark fibers in a square make two paper. The "Paper" research unlocks it, together with the sugar cane paper recipe.
- Bark fiber burns as fuel for as long as a stick, in furnaces, smokers, blast furnaces and the combustion generator. Hoppers and item conduits put it into the fuel slot.
- Removed the bark fiber string recipe. String now comes from infested leaves.
